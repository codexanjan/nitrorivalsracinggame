# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-06

First packaged GitHub release of Nitro Rivals.

- Three 3D racing locations with Sprint, Circuit and Time Trial modes.
- Four unlockable cars and six distinct selectable drivers.
- Five AI rivals with equal vehicle performance and Pro difficulty by default.
- Solid car contacts, jump clearance, ramps, nitro effects and crash particles.
- Chase, hood and cockpit views with live dashboard, GPS and rear-view mirror.
- Brake/reverse controls, safe respawn, repair kits and nitro canisters.
- Saved coins, car upgrades, paint choices and personal bests.
- Trophy-holding champion ceremony with red carpet and confetti.
- Original synthesized racing soundtrack and sound effects.
- Illustrated README, sharing kit, contribution guide and seven regression suites.
